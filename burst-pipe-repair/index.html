Home / Services / Burst Pipe Repair

A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

Davenport Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Davenport and surrounding areas. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line
  • Wrap any bare pipe that runs through a crawl space, garage or attic without heat
  • Before winter, drain outside spigots and take the hoses off them
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and how to operate it quickly
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes

Why Burst Pipe Repair Is Not a DIY Job

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. It moves between ceiling joists, inside floor assemblies, beside electrical conduit and down wall cavities, soaking materials you might not find for days or even weeks. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.

Warning Signs for Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • Exposed pipes that drip, mist or shoot water
  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
