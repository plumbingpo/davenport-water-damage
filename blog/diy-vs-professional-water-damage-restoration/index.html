When to Hire a Water Damage Professional in Davenport, IA

Home / Blog / Should You DIY Water Damage Cleanup in Davenport, IA

When water damage strikes your Davenport, IA home, one of the first decisions is whether to handle the cleanup yourself or call a professional restoration company. The instinct to save money by doing it yourself is understandable, but most water damage is far more complex than it appears on the surface. Read on to learn where the line sits between a do-it-yourself job and one that calls for professional restoration.

Jobs You May Be Able to Handle Yourself

A minor spill that stays in one spot can sometimes be handled on your own. Say a sink overflows with clean water, wets a hard, non-porous floor over an area smaller than about 10 square feet, and is cleaned up inside of an hour: that is probably a job for you. Likewise, you most likely can skip the professional when a minor spill on a tile or vinyl floor is wiped up at once and dried with a dehumidifier and fans. What matters is a small affected area, a clean source, a fast cleanup, and no significant wetting of anything porous.

When Professional Help Is Essential

In practice, the majority of water losses need a professional restoration crew. Call a professional right away if the affected area is larger than 10 square feet, water has been sitting for more than a few hours, water contacted carpet, drywall, or other porous materials, the source is contaminated (gray or black water), water may have entered wall cavities or structural components, you cannot identify or stop the source, or you notice musty odors suggesting mold has begun.

The Hidden Costs of DIY Water Damage Cleanup

Many Davenport homeowners who attempt DIY water damage cleanup end up spending more than they would have with professional restoration. Here is why: incomplete drying leads to mold growth within days, adding $1,500 to $6,000 or more in remediation costs. Replacement costs also climb, because anything left damp continues to deteriorate. The cost of rented extraction and drying gear, fans and dehumidifiers included, mounts quickly while performing below a professional setup. If the cleanup is poorly documented, your insurance payout may be smaller. Meanwhile the damage keeps compounding, since proper restoration cannot start until the unsuccessful DIY effort ends.

What a Professional Crew Brings

Here is what you gain by hiring professionals instead of doing it yourself: confidence in a job done right, detailed documentation for your insurer, antimicrobial products that help stop mold, moisture monitoring to verify everything is fully dry, thermal cameras for locating hidden moisture, commercial equipment that dries wall cavities as well as structural materials, and truck-mounted extractors that pull water out hundreds of times faster.
