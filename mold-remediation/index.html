Effective mold remediation goes far beyond scrubbing visible mold off surfaces. Mold Remediation Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • When windows, piping or other cold surfaces start to sweat, correct it without waiting
  • Mold-resistant paint and wallboard are the better choice wherever moisture is common, such as a basement or bath
  • Make sure bathrooms, kitchens and laundry areas have adequate exhaust ventilation
  • Mold can get going after just 24 to 48 hours of dampness, so have leaks fixed within 24 hours
  • Aim for 30 to 50% indoor humidity and never let it stay above 60%, running dehumidifiers and ventilating as needed
  • A vapor barrier is worth considering in a crawl space, which also needs correct ventilation

How to Tell You Need Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water damage not long ago that was left to dry without professional equipment
  • Signs of concealed moisture such as paint that bubbles, wallpaper that peels or materials that warp
  • An earthy, musty smell that will not go away, mostly in humid or closed-off spaces
  • Headaches, allergy flare-ups or breathing symptoms that ease whenever you leave the house
  • Discovering a plumbing or roof leak that has been dripping for a long time
  • Cleaning that never lasts, with growth returning to the same surface
  • Black, green, white or other colored growth you can see on surfaces in the building

Why Professional Mold Remediation Matters

Mold remediation is not a DIY project. When a colony is disturbed and nothing contains it, millions of spores go into the air, where they may settle in clean rooms and expose your family more than the mold did before. Capturing and containing the spores during removal takes HEPA-filtered scrubbers, protective equipment for the crew and containment kept under negative air pressure, which is how professionals do it.

Professional mold remediation also addresses the root cause of the growth, which is the moisture source. Removing visible mold without correcting the underlying moisture problem means the mold will return, often within weeks. Certified remediation technicians have the training and equipment to identify hidden moisture sources, dry affected areas properly and put solutions in place that prevent recurrence.
